04 / FAMILY SYSTEMS PSYCHOTHERAPY

家族システム精神療法を中心とした診療

Zendo Health Center（ZHC）では、家族システム精神療法を中心とした診療を行います。

精神科医マレー・ボーエン博士が確立した「家族システムズ理論」の考え方を取り入れ、こころの問題を個人だけのものとして捉えず、家族との関係や生活環境も含めて考えます。ご本人の精神的な成長と、より安定した人間関係づくりを支援します。

04 / FAMILY SYSTEMS PSYCHOTHERAPY

A Family Systems Psychotherapy-Centered Approach

At Zendo Health Center (ZHC), care centers on family systems psychotherapy.

Drawing on Family Systems Theory, developed by psychiatrist Murray Bowen, we consider emotional difficulties in the context of family relationships and living circumstances, rather than viewing them solely as individual problems. This approach supports personal emotional growth and the development of more stable relationships.

We aim to avoid medication whenever possible and keep its use to a minimum when it is essential. When medication may be appropriate, its purpose, potential benefits, and alternatives are explained carefully. The need for medication is assessed thoughtfully in consultation with each client.
